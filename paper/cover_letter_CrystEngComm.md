Please consider our manuscript, "From Topological Design to Physically Instantiable MOF Candidates: Symmetry-Aware Enumeration and Reconstruction", for publication as a Research Article in *CrystEngComm*.

Designing useful MOF screening sets remains difficult because large topological search spaces contain substantial symmetry redundancy, while graph-level assignments do not necessarily remain viable once oriented building units are reconstructed as periodic crystal models. In this work, we present a deterministic symmetry-aware workflow that combines non-redundant enumeration with rigid-body reconstruction into explicit framework models.

The importance of this work is that it offers a practical bridge between topological design and crystal-level candidate evaluation. In benchmark cases, the enumeration handles spaces of up to about 5 x 10^5 configurations with orbit counts consistent with Burnside verification and exhaustive CASM checks. The accepted assignments can be reconstructed as sanitizable periodic CIFs across representative MOF topologies, and fixed-composition single-point DFT examples further show that the symmetry-prioritized search retains competitive low-energy candidates within the sampled screening space. We believe this contribution is well suited to *CrystEngComm* because it connects candidate generation, framework reconstruction, and early-stage computational assessment in a crystal-engineering context.

This manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to *CrystEngComm*.
